## Deliberately not advertised

Target selection, sacrifice selection, offline recovery, Echo recognition, and ARGUS recognition were not live-verified in the available evidence set. The commercial uses no claim that these workflows are currently available or successful.

## Representation disclosure

Every frame in this production is original motion graphics and is labeled `GAMEPLAY VISUALIZATION`. It is not a literal capture of the app UI and does not claim that the app renders holographic cards.
