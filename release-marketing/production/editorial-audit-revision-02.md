# BoardState Lite Advertisement Editorial Audit

## Review scope

The previous advertisement revision was archived at `archive/previous-ad/` before the new edit. The earlier cut was readable as a visual proof-of-concept, but it relied too heavily on title cards, repeated life-counter footage, and synthetic narration. It did not give the viewer enough time to understand the resulting battlefield changes, and its advanced-feature claims were broader than the live evidence.

## Keep / reshoot / remove

| Source | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `raw/verified-marketing/core-feature-pass.webm` | KEEP | Authentic 430x900 application capture. Shows life, Full-Screen Life, deterministic counter handling, Ability Focus, a once-per-turn ability, and Undo context. |
| `raw/verified-marketing/dungeon-initiative-pass.webm` | KEEP | Authentic User Tools to Dungeons & Initiative workflow with initiative and Secret Entrance visible. |
| `raw/final-ad/*` | REMOVE FROM MARKETING CUT | The target/sacrifice path did not expose a working target selector in the live application. It is retained as engineering evidence, not advertised. |
| Previous cinematic renders | ARCHIVE | Preserved unchanged under `archive/previous-ad/`; not used as the final revision source. |
| Synthetic narration from the earlier cut | REMOVE | The revised cut is intentionally delivered without placeholder AI narration. A performance-ready human narration script is included. |

## Editorial changes

The revision uses slower, result-first shots, large portrait application framing, purpose-built landscape and vertical compositions, restrained feature headlines, and a shorter feature list tied to passing evidence. It omits target selection, sacrifice selection, Offline recovery, Echo, and ARGUS from promotional claims because those workflows were not successfully verified in the available runtime.
